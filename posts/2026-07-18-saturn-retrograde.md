Saturn's backward journey through Pisces asks us to review our boundaries, dreams, and spiritual commitments. This is not a punishment — it's a renovation. Saturn is the architect of the zodiac, and retrograde periods are when he checks the blueprints.

## What Saturn Retrograde Means

When Saturn goes retrograde, the external pressure eases slightly, but the internal work intensifies. This is a time for reflection, not action. The structures Saturn has been building since it entered Pisces in March 2023 are being stress-tested. What's solid will stand. What's shaky needs reinforcement.

## Pisces Themes Under Review

- **Boundaries:** Where are you letting others drain your energy? Where are you escaping responsibility through fantasy?
- **Spiritual Practice:** Is your spiritual life grounded, or is it just escapism? Saturn demands discipline even in the mystical.
- **Compassion vs. Martyrdom:** Pisces loves to sacrifice, but Saturn asks: Are you helping, or are you enabling?
- **Creative Projects:** Dreams need structure to become reality. What have you been procrastinating on?

## How to Work With This Energy

1. **Audit your commitments:** What have you agreed to that no longer aligns? Saturn retrograde is the perfect time to renegotiate or release.
2. **Rebuild your spiritual practice:** If your meditation, prayer, or ritual practice has become sporadic, reinstate discipline.
3. **Face the fear:** Pisces rules the unconscious. What have you been avoiding? Saturn retrograde brings it to the surface so you can finally deal with it.

- **Saturn Retrograde Ends:** November 15, 2026

This retrograde is a gift disguised as hard work. Use it wisely.
