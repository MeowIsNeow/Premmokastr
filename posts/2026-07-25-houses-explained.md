Think of houses as the stage where planetary actors perform. Each house represents a different area of your life, and the planets that land in each house bring their energy to that specific domain. Without understanding houses, you're reading a script without knowing the scene.

## The House System

There are twelve houses in every natal chart, numbered counterclockwise starting from the Ascendant (your rising sign). Each house covers approximately 30 degrees of the zodiac wheel, though house systems can vary in size depending on which system you use (Placidus, Whole Sign, Equal, etc.).

## Quick Reference Guide

| House | Area of Life | Keywords |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1st | Self, identity, appearance | "I am" |
| 2nd | Money, values, possessions | "I have" |
| 3rd | Communication, siblings, short trips | "I think" |
| 4th | Home, family, roots | "I feel" |
| 5th | Creativity, romance, children | "I create" |
| 6th | Health, daily routines, service | "I serve" |
| 7th | Partnerships, marriage, contracts | "I relate" |
| 8th | Transformation, shared resources, death | "I transform" |
| 9th | Higher learning, travel, philosophy | "I understand" |
| 10th | Career, public image, authority | "I achieve" |
| 11th | Friends, groups, hopes | "I dream" |
| 12th | Secrets, spirituality, hidden enemies | "I surrender" |

## How to Use This

When you look at your natal chart, notice which planets fall in which houses. A Venus in the 7th house suggests love comes through partnership. A Mars in the 10th house drives career ambition. The house gives the *where*; the planet gives the *what*.

Start with your Sun and Moon placements. Where do they live in your chart? That's where your core identity and emotional needs express themselves most strongly.
