The North Node isn't just a point in your chart — it's a compass pointing toward your highest evolution in this lifetime. While your South Node represents the skills, habits, and comfort zones you've already mastered (often in past lives), your North Node is the unfamiliar territory your soul came here to explore.

## What Is the North Node?

In astrology, the North Node (or True Node) is one of the two lunar nodes — the points where the Moon's orbit intersects the ecliptic. The South Node is directly opposite. Together, they form the Nodal Axis, one of the most spiritually significant placements in your natal chart.

Your North Node sign reveals the qualities you need to develop, while the house placement shows the life area where this growth happens.

## Why It Matters

Most of us instinctively gravitate toward our South Node. It feels easy, natural, comfortable. But staying there leads to stagnation. The North Node feels awkward at first — like learning a new language — but it's where your deepest fulfillment lives.

## Quick Guide by Sign

- **North Node in Aries:** Learn to be independent, assertive, and self-focused. Your path is about courage and initiative.
- **North Node in Taurus:** Develop stability, patience, and self-worth. Build something lasting.
- **North Node in Gemini:** Embrace curiosity, communication, and connection. Share your ideas.
- **North Node in Cancer:** Nurture yourself and others. Allow vulnerability and emotional depth.

The North Node is not about abandoning your past — it's about integrating it while bravely stepping into the new.
