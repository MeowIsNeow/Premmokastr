You don't need hours to connect with the cosmos. Here are five simple rituals to weave astrology into your everyday routine — no advanced knowledge required.

## 1. Morning Moon Check (2 minutes)

Before you check your phone, ask: What phase is the moon in today? What sign is it in? The moon changes signs every 2–3 days, coloring the emotional tone of each day. A moon in Aries feels very different from a moon in Taurus. Simply knowing this helps you align your energy with the cosmic weather.

**Pro tip:** Keep a lunar calendar on your desk or use a moon phase app.

## 2. Track Your Transits (5 minutes weekly)

Once a week, look up what major transits are happening. Is Mercury changing signs? Is there a full moon coming? Is Mars aspecting your natal Venus? You don't need to understand every detail — just notice the patterns. Over time, you'll start to feel the correlations between cosmic movements and your lived experience.

## 3. Journal with Intention (10 minutes)

Use astrology as a journaling prompt. Some ideas:
- "Today the moon is in Scorpio. What am I ready to release?"
- "Mercury is retrograde. What old project needs revisiting?"
- "It's a new moon in my 10th house. What career intention can I set?"

## 4. Weekly Chart Review (15 minutes)

Spend 15 minutes each Sunday looking at your natal chart. Pick one planet and study it. Where is it? What house? What aspects does it make? What does it mean? Deep knowledge comes from slow, repeated exposure — not cramming.

## 5. Set an Astrological Alarm (1 minute)

Set a recurring calendar reminder for major astrological events: solstices, equinoxes, new moons, full moons, eclipses. When the reminder pops up, take one intentional action — light a candle, say an affirmation, or simply pause and breathe.

## The Secret

Consistency beats intensity. Five minutes a day for a year will teach you more than a weekend workshop. The cosmos is always speaking — the question is whether we're listening.
