For the next month, Venus in Leo brings passion, romance, and a flair for the dramatic to relationships across all signs. Leo is the heart of the zodiac — generous, warm, and unapologetically expressive. When Venus, the planet of love and beauty, moves through this fire sign, we all feel the heat.

## What Venus in Leo Means

Venus in Leo doesn't do subtle. This transit is about grand gestures, public declarations of love, and showing up fully in your relationships. If you've been holding back emotionally, Leo energy says: let them see your heart.

This is also an incredibly creative transit. Leo rules self-expression, and Venus here amplifies artistic inspiration. Whether you're a painter, writer, musician, or simply someone who wants to redecorate your space — this is your moment.

## For Each Element

- **Fire Signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius):** This is your season to shine. Romance, creativity, and confidence are all amplified. Take the lead in love.
- **Earth Signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn):** Let yourself be a little more playful. Leo energy helps you loosen up and enjoy life more.
- **Air Signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius):** Communication in relationships gets warmer and more heartfelt. Say what you feel.
- **Water Signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces):** Your emotional depth meets Leo's warmth. This is a beautiful time for deepening intimacy.

## Dates to Remember

- **Venus enters Leo:** August 1, 2026
- **Venus leaves Leo:** August 26, 2026

Don't play small with your heart during this transit. Leo energy rewards the brave.
